01What is Pem?

Every streaming service has an algorithm for you, but none of them plans for your household. Pem changes that by combining everyone's tastes, watch lists and subscriptions into one simple, personalized TV guide for the time you spend watching together.

Whether you have an hour or a whole evening, Pem builds a lineup everyone can enjoy. No endless scrolling, no debating what to watch. Just more time enjoying TV together.

02Why should I use Pem?
  • Better together. Pem combines everyone's tastes and watch lists to find entertainment the whole household can enjoy.
  • Less scrolling, more watching. Stop spending half the evening deciding what to watch. Pem already has a plan.
  • Make the most of your time. Whether you have 45 minutes or three hours, Pem creates a lineup that fits your evening.
  • Take turns without keeping score. Pem balances everyone's preferences so one person's favorites don't always win.
  • Your watch list, finally watched. Turn that ever-growing list of things you've been meaning to watch into an actual plan.
  • Discover something new. Pem's Picks introduce you to great shows and movies you might never have chosen as a household.
03How does it work?

Each person in your household keeps their own lists and ratings in the iPhone app, which teaches Pem what they like. When you sit down to watch together, open Pem on Apple TV to see the lineup it planned for you. Fewer options, chosen in less time, and pressing Watch opens the right streaming app.

04Which streaming services does it work with?

The ones you already pay for, including Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV, HBO Max, Disney+, Hulu, Peacock, Paramount+ and more. Pem plans across all of them at once, so you don't have to open each app to see what's there.

05What will happen with my data?

It stays with your household. Pem never asks for your streaming passwords or reads your accounts, and it will never sell your viewing data. Pem's recommendations are never for sale, either.
